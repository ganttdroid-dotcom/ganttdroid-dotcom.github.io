Last updated: October 2, 2026

GanttDroid ("the app") is a project management app with Gantt charts for Android. This policy explains what information the app handles and why. The app is published by the GanttDroid developer, contact: ganttdroid@gmail.com.

Your projects stay on your device

Projects, tasks, resources, calendars and templates are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. The app has no user accounts and no server of its own: we never receive your projects. Files you open, import, export or share (for example GanttProject .gan files or images of a chart) are read or written only where you choose, through Android's file picker or share sheet, and handled by the apps you pick.

Advertising

The free version shows banner ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares with Google information such as your device's advertising ID, device and app information (model, operating system, app version), approximate location derived from your IP address, and interactions with ads, plus diagnostic data. This happens under Google's own terms: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland the app asks for your consent first, using Google's consent form. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings. Buying "Remove ads" in the app removes the ads.

Purchases

In-app purchases ("Remove ads", "Templates") are processed by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details. The app only checks with Google Play which products you own, on your device.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect your name, email, contacts, precise location or files.
  • We do not sell personal information.
  • The app does not use analytics or crash reporting services.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.

Deleting your data

Everything the app stores is on your device: deleting a project in the app, clearing the app's data, or uninstalling the app removes it. Data collected by Google for advertising is governed by Google's privacy policy and your Google account and device settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.

Política de privacidad (español)

Última actualización: 2 de octubre de 2026

GanttDroid («la app») es una app de gestión de proyectos con diagramas de Gantt para Android. Contacto: ganttdroid@gmail.com.

Tus proyectos se quedan en tu dispositivo

Los proyectos, tareas, recursos, calendarios y plantillas se guardan solo en el almacenamiento privado de la app en tu dispositivo. La app no tiene cuentas de usuario ni servidor propio: nunca recibimos tus proyectos. Los ficheros que abres, importas, exportas o compartes solo se leen o escriben donde tú eliges, mediante el selector de ficheros o el menú de compartir de Android.

Publicidad

La versión gratuita muestra anuncios de Google AdMob. Para mostrarlos y medirlos, y para evitar fraudes, el SDK de anuncios de Google recoge y comparte con Google datos como el ID de publicidad del dispositivo, información del dispositivo y de la app, la ubicación aproximada deducida de la dirección IP, las interacciones con los anuncios y datos de diagnóstico, según las condiciones de Google. En el Espacio Económico Europeo, el Reino Unido y Suiza la app te pide antes tu consentimiento con el formulario de Google; puedes cambiarlo cuando quieras en Ajustes → Opciones de privacidad. Comprar «Quitar anuncios» los elimina.

Compras

Las compras dentro de la app las procesa Google Play. No recibimos tus datos de pago.

Lo que no hacemos

No recogemos tu nombre, correo, contactos, ubicación precisa ni ficheros; no vendemos datos personales; la app no usa servicios de analítica ni de informes de errores. La app no está dirigida a menores de 13 años.

Borrar tus datos

Todo lo que guarda la app está en tu dispositivo: borrar un proyecto, borrar los datos de la app o desinstalarla lo elimina.
